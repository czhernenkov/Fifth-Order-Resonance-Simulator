The detuning δ is set so (Jfp, ψfp) is an island centre. Q₀ = 1/5 + δ is the bare tune used for D₅; nonlinear detuning brings the fixed point onto the 1/5 resonance.

Hamiltonian and one-turn dynamics

H = 2πδJ + (α/2)J² + V₅J5/2cos(5ψ)
dJ/dn = 5V₅J5/2sin(5ψ)
dψ/dn = 2πδ + αJ + (5/2)V₅J3/2cos(5ψ)

The fast frame rotation R(2π/5) is removed during integration; R(2π/5)⁵ = I. The displayed stroboscopic phase-space samples are taken once every five turns, when the laboratory and rotating-frame angles coincide modulo 2π.

Coefficient correspondence

α = 2πξ
κeff(Jfp) = V₅Jfp5/2
QI ≈ (5/2π)Jfp5/4√|αV₅|

Unlike a constant κ kick, the decapole drive scales as J5/2. Its action derivative also contributes to the angle equation.
